- **Text fields: keyed highlights and links in a field of more than one paragraph are found and drawn where their paragraph lays them out.** A wrapping field that lays out by paragraph left them out of `Runtime::find`, `Tx::scroll_into_view` and `Cx::layout` reads, and drew each highlight, link paint and highlight colour from the whole text's bytes in every paragraph, so one past the first paragraph drew in the wrong place or not at all. Each paragraph now takes the highlights and links over its own bytes, moved onto them as its spans and strikethroughs are.
