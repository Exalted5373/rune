- **Text fields: links in a field of more than one paragraph are found and drawn where their paragraph lays them out.** A wrapping field that lays out by paragraph left its links out of `Cx::layout` reads and press targets, and drew each link's paint and colour from the whole text's bytes in every paragraph, so a link past the first paragraph drew in the wrong place or not at all. Each paragraph now takes the links over its own bytes, as #1057 does for highlights.
