- **`Tx::open_files` picks several files in one Open dialog.** Its callback gets every file chosen (`FilesDialogResult`, empty when cancelled): rfd's `pick_files` on macOS and Windows, the XDG portal FileChooser's `multiple` choice on Linux. `Runtime::file_dialog_multiple` and `Runtime::resolve_file_dialog_paths` drive it in tests, and `--tree` marks it `"multiple": true`. `open_file`, `save_file` and `open_folder` are unchanged.
