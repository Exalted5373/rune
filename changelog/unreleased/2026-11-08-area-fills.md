- **Shapes fill areas with holes.** `Area::new(&path, FillRule::EvenOdd)` (or `NonZero`) fills all of a `Path`'s contours together, as SVG's `fill-rule` does: an outline with holes cut out, a group of islands, contours that cross. It is tessellated once, when it is made, into the seamless triangles fills already draw with, and its clones share them, so keep areas in state and draw them with `Shape::fill_area`; moving or scaling the element reuses the triangles. `Area::contains` hit-tests a point by the same rule, so a point in a hole misses. `Shape::fill_path_with` tessellates and fills in one call, and `Path::polygon` adds a closed contour from a list of points. `Shape::fill` and `Shape::fill_path` are unchanged.
