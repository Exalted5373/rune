- **Per-span families and features, and app script fonts.** `Span::family` shapes a span in its own family (a `Family::Named` that isn't registered loads from the system, as an element's does) and `Span::features` adds OpenType features on top of the element's, a span's tag winning over the same tag there; both reach the web export's HTML. `rune::text::set_script_fonts` (and `TextSystem::set_script_fonts`) maps scripts such as `Hebr`, `Grek`, `Arab`, `Syrc` and `Ethi` to the app's bundled families, tried after the element's own families and before the monospace, symbol and system fallbacks. `Span` gains the public fields `family` and `features`: a `Span { .. }` literal that names every field needs them, one that ends in `..Default::default()` or starts from `Span::new` doesn't. Shaping tests cover pointed Hebrew, vocalized Arabic, polytonic Greek, Syriac and Ge'ez in bundled subsets, and `examples/interlinear.rs` shows them with an interlinear under `El::dir(Direction::Rtl)`.
