- **Right-to-left rows, per-span families and features, app script fonts and an explicit text direction.** `El::rtl` lays a row's children out right to left (each `flex_wrap` line fills from the right, lines run down); presses follow what's drawn, Tab keeps reading order, and Left and Right swap in a `focus_group` the row lays out. `Span::family` and `Span::features` shape a span in its own family and add OpenType features on top of the element's. `rune::text::set_script_fonts` (and `TextSystem::set_script_fonts`) maps scripts such as `Hebr`, `Grek`, `Arab`, `Syrc` and `Ethi` to the app's bundled families, tried after the element's own families and before the system fonts. `El::text_direction` and `RichLayoutOptions::direction` set a paragraph's base direction (`TextDirection::Auto`, `Ltr`, `Rtl`). `Span` has two new public fields, `family` and `features`: a literal listing every field needs `..Span::new(..)`. Nothing changes for apps that don't use them.
